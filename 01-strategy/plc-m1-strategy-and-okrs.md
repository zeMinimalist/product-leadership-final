# Product Strategy One-Pager & OKRs, Module 1

- **Scenario:** Meridian Foundations (B2B)

## Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| Winning aspiration | Be the tool US commercial-construction superintendents reach for when a site issue threatens progress: capture it once, get it to the responsible person, and know it’s resolved without chasing group texts or reentering updates. |
| Where to play | - US general contractors already using Meridian on commercial projects worth $50M or more. Serve field superintendents, with subcontractor foremen participating in issue resolution.
- Start with one mobile workflow: photo → location and issue → responsible person → resolution confirmation. Reach users through existing customer relationships and job-site onboarding. |
| How to win | - Combine the convenience of a phone camera with Meridian’s existing project context and enterprise record. A field update should become a traceable, assigned issue without a second administrative step.
- Our potential advantage is the connection between field action and the enterprise system already in use, not photo capture itself. We must validate that existing project data is accurate and accessible enough to make this easier than texting. |
| Capabilities required | - Build: fast, offline-capable capture and reliable synchronization; simple assignment and resolution tracking.
- Reuse: existing project records, permissions, and document-control infrastructure.
- Buy where practical: commodity mobile services such as push notifications.
- Partner with pilot customers: UX research and customer success observe real job-site work and remove onboarding friction. |
| Management systems | - Weekly pilot review: repeat use by superintendent cohort, share of eligible issues handled end-to-end, and capture-to-assignment time.
- Monthly outcome review: time spent chasing updates, duplicate entry, and unresolved-issue age.
- Enterprise guardrails: synchronization reliability and preservation of required permissions and records.
- Decision rule: expand only when repeat field use and reduced coordination effort improve together. Establish baselines before setting numerical targets. |

## One hard no
We will not build advanced enterprise analytics in this initiative. Those capabilities may help office teams, but they do not solve the superintendent’s immediate coordination problem and would split the 10-person team’s focus. Existing enterprise functionality remains intact.

## OKR cascade
- **Objective:** - Make Meridian Foundations the default way field superintendents on existing US commercial projects worth $50M or more coordinate site issues—without chasing group texts or entering the same update twice. - Measurement scope: pilot job sites; a 12-week evaluation period. The numerical baselines below are illustrative planning assumptions, not Meridian data. Validate them before treating these OKRs as committed targets.
- **KR1:** Workflow adoption: increase the share of eligible site issues coordinated end-to-end in Meridian, from capture through confirmed resolution, from an assumed 10% to a proposed 60% by the end of week 12.
- **KR2:** Less chasing: reduce superintendent time spent chasing ownership and status updates from an assumed 60 to a proposed 30 minutes per workday by the end of week 12.
- **KR3:** No duplicate entry: increase the share of field-captured issues available in the enterprise record without manual reentry from an assumed 20% to a proposed 90% by the end of week 12.
